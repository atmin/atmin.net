What you can count on

No plaintext on the server

Messages are encrypted on your device before they leave it. The server only ever sees ciphertext it cannot read.

Find people, not phone numbers

Sign up with a handle you choose — no phone, no email, no address-book upload. Opt-in private discovery is on the native-apps track; even then, your contacts never leave your device.

Your keys, your history

Keys and history live on your devices, and the server keeps an encrypted backup only your password can open. Forget it and there is no reset — by design.

Sync-first, offline-friendly

Messages sync from your encrypted mailbox. The client is explicit about what has been delivered and what is still pending.
